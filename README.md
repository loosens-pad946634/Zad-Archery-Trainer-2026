## 🎮 About the Game

Zad Archery is a single-player incremental action RPG and strategy game developed and published by Samharia Studios. It released on September 1, 2026, and centers on shooting waves of target-like monsters, collecting loot, and improving an archer through skills, jobs, crafting, mining, and other progression systems.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Zad Archery**. This trainer enhances Zad Archery's incremental progression loop with options for faster combat, expanded resources, improved crafting, skill progression, and easier experimentation with the game's Archer Jobs and upgrade systems.

**Target Audience:** Single-player users who want to speed up incremental progression, experiment with skills and Archer Jobs, test crafting and mining systems, or reduce repetitive resource and wave-grinding.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Zad Archery uses mouse-driven interaction rather than conventional free-roaming movement. Use the mouse to select targets, interact with menus, manage upgrades, activate abilities, and progress through available systems. The trainer does not add movement controls.

### Tools & Inventory

Collect loot from defeated target monsters and use gathered materials for equipment, upgrades, and crafting. Open the relevant inventory or crafting interface before enabling resource, loot, or free-crafting options. Avoid changing item quantities while a transaction is actively processing.

### World & Environment

Progress through waves of target-like monsters and unlock additional systems as your archer becomes stronger. Mining and other progression activities provide resources for upgrades, while jobs, masteries, guardian perks, and shaping expand long-term character development.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat focuses on ranged attacks against successive waves of enemies. Select targets with the mouse, use available active skills, and improve performance through equipment, passive upgrades, jobs, and mining-related enhancements. Enable damage, cooldown, health, or wave-clearing options before starting a difficult encounter.
